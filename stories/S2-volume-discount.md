# S2. Volume discount

As a shopper buying in bulk, I want a discount on products I buy many units of.

## Acceptance criteria

- **S2-AC1** 10 or more units of the same product (after merging lines) take 10% off that line.
- **S2-AC2** 50 or more units take 15% off that line instead.
- **S2-AC3** Products in the `books` category never get a volume discount.
