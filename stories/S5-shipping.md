# S5. Shipping

As a shopper, I want to know what delivery costs, and to get it free on bigger orders.

## Acceptance criteria

- **S5-AC1** Standard shipping costs 4.99, and is free when the goods, after all discounts, come to 50.00 or more.
- **S5-AC2** Express shipping costs 9.99 and is never free.
- **S5-AC3** Any other shipping method raises `CartError`.
